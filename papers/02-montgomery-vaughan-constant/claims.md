## Reasoning

### #2

Inequality (1) must hold for all admissible data with one constant C, so C is at least the ratio for any one choice. For w = a + ib with real a, b, the form Σ\_{m≠n} w\_m conj(w\_n)/(λ\_m − λ\_n) has real part Σ(a\_m a\_n + b\_m b\_n)/(λ\_m − λ\_n). The kernel is antisymmetric, so this real part is 0. The imaginary part is Σ(b\_m a\_n − a\_m b\_n)/(λ\_m − λ\_n) = 2T. With integer data every term is rational. Integer positions lose no generality, by affine invariance.

### #3 to #6

The ratio is evaluated by two independent programs, in different languages with different arithmetic.

1. **C program** with `__float128` and an explicit rounding-error bound, (2N + 4)u Σ|terms| with u = 2⁻¹¹³.
2. **python-flint** Arb ball arithmetic over all N² pair terms.

They agree to 27 digits, far below the margins above π. A third implementation, in binary64 with an a-priori bound and exact fraction comparisons, agrees to 16 digits.

The weights come from a floating-point eigenvector, but that does not matter for validity. Any admissible data give a valid lower bound, and the data file is the object that is verified.
